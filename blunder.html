    How it works

    A few moves from one of your own games play out on the board. One of your moves there was a blunder: it threw away a good part of the game. Click the move you think it was. Two wrong guesses and the answer is shown, with the move that was there instead. The games come from your analysed games and the mistakes found on your puzzles.
