Damiano Defence Trap

A trap for White in the King's Pawn Game: Damiano Defense. Black's natural-looking fxe5 gives White a winning attack on the open king.

The moves

How it works

After ...f6, Nxe5! gives the knight. Taking it opens the e8-h5 diagonal: Qh5+ and the king has to walk out.

What it wins

White gets a winning attack on the open king.

How Black avoids it

Taking the knight opens the king: Qh5+ and it has to walk out. Hit the knight with ...Qe7 instead and win back the pawn. The safe move is Qe7.
