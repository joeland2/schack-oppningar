Légal's Mate

A trap for White in the Philidor Defense. Black's natural-looking Bxd1 gives White mate.

The moves

How it works

The pin on the f3 knight is not real. Nxe5 offers the queen, and taking it walks into Bxf7+ and Nd5#.

What it wins

White gets mate.

How Black avoids it

It looks harmless, but White plays Nxe5! anyway: if you take the queen, Bxf7+ and Nd5# is mate. The safe move is Nf6.
