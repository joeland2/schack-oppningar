Kieninger Trap

A trap for Black in the Indian Defense: Budapest Gambit Accepted, Main Line, Adler Variation. White's natural-looking 8.axb4 gives Black mate.

The moves

How it works

In the Budapest, taking the bishop on b4 opens d3 for the knight: ...Nd3# while the queen pins the e-pawn.

What it wins

Black gets mate.
