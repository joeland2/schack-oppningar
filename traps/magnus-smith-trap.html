Magnus Smith Trap

A trap for White in the Sicilian Defense: Classical Variation, Sozin Attack. Black's natural-looking dxe5 gives White the queen.

The moves

How it works

In the Sicilian, e5 hits the knight. Taking with ...dxe5 opens the d-file: Bxf7+ Kxf7 and Qxd8.

What it wins

White gets the queen.

How Black avoids it

Taking on e5 opens the d-file under your queen: Bxf7+ Kxf7 Qxd8. Move the knight with ...Nd7 instead. The safe move is Nd7.
