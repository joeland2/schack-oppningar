Caro-Kann Smothered Mate

A trap for White in the Caro-Kann Defense. Black's natural-looking Ngf6 gives White mate.

The moves

How it works

With the queen on e2, the knight on d7 blocks the king's last square. Developing the other knight allows Nd6#.

What it wins

White gets mate.

How Black avoids it

The knight on d7 blocks the king's last square, and the queen on e2 pins the e-pawn: Nd6 is mate. The safe move is Ndf6.
