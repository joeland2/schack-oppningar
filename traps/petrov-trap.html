Petrov Trap

A trap for White in the Petrov's Defense: Damiano Variation. Black's natural-looking Nf6 gives White the queen.

The moves

How it works

After 3...Nxe4? Qe2 pins on the e-file. Moving the knight away opens the file: Nc6+ is a discovered check that hits the queen.

What it wins

White gets the queen.

How Black avoids it

Taking back the pawn at once walks into Qe2: the knight is pinned, and when it moves, Nc6+ uncovers check and wins the queen. Chase the knight with ...d6 first. The safe move is d6.
