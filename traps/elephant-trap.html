Elephant Trap

A trap for Black in the Queen's Gambit Declined: Modern Variation, Knight Defense. White's natural-looking 6.Nxd5 gives Black a piece.

The moves

How it works

The knight on f6 only looks pinned. Nxd5? is met by ...Nxd5!, and Bxd8 Bb4+ gets the queen back with a piece to spare.

What it wins

Black gets a piece.
