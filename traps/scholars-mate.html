Scholar's Mate

A trap for White in the Bishop's Opening. Black's natural-looking Nf6 gives White mate.

The moves

How it works

The queen and bishop both hit f7. Developing the knight to f6 to chase the queen forgets the threat: Qxf7#.

What it wins

White gets mate.

How Black avoids it

Qh5 and Bc4 both hit f7. Block the queen with ...g6 (or guard with ...Qe7) before chasing it. The safe move is g6.
