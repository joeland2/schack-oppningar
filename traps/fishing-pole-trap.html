Fishing Pole Trap

A trap for Black in the Ruy Lopez: Berlin Defense, Fishing Pole Variation. White's natural-looking 6.hxg4 gives Black mate.

The moves

How it works

Taking the knight on g4 opens the h-file: ...hxg4 hits the knight on f3, and ...Qh4 with the rook behind it mates on h1 or h2.

What it wins

Black gets mate.

How White avoids it

The knight on g4 is bait: hxg4? hxg4 opens the h-file, and ...Qh4 mates. Leave it and play on with c3 or d4. The safe move is c3.
